Technology

THE INSTRUMENTS BEHIND THE ACCURACY.

TOTAL STATION

Precision angle and distance measurement with electronic data capture for boundary, topographic and construction layout surveys.

GNSS / DGPS

High-accuracy satellite positioning with differential corrections for rapid site setting out and control network establishment.

THEODOLITE

Reliable horizontal and vertical angle measurement for traverse networks, alignment checks and structural monitoring.

AUTOCAD

Professional DWG drafting — plans, sections, layouts and detail drawings delivered in your layer standards and templates.

ARCGIS

Digital mapping and spatial analysis — geodatabases, feature classes and web maps ready for your GIS workflow.

CIVIL 3D

Engineering-ready terrain models, corridors, pipe networks and grading — surfaces that calculate volumes and design intent.

Services

WHAT WE SURVEY

Every job is handed back as a drawing your engineer or authority can act on directly.

01

PLOT & BOUNDARY DEMARCATION

Precise identification and marking of property lines, so ownership and extent are clear before you build, sell, or settle a dispute.

02

TOPOGRAPHIC & CONTOUR SURVEY

Elevation and terrain mapping used for drainage, earthwork and site planning, delivered as contour-ready CAD and GIS files.

03

ROAD & ALIGNMENT SURVEY

Centre-line, widening and alignment surveys for road and infrastructure work, referenced to fixed benchmarks.

04

PILING & LAYOUT SETTING OUT

Pile point marking and construction layout using DGPS, set out to the drawing before the first stake goes into the ground.

05

CONSTRUCTION & BUILDING SURVEY

Levelling and layout support through a build, plus surveys required for permits and completion certificates.

06

CAD & GIS DRAFTING OUTPUT

Field data processed into clean AutoCAD, ArcGIS and Civil 3D drawings — ready for your architect, engineer or local authority.
